But a dossier cannot claim bit-for-bit integrity for a receipt hash that is no longer the active receipt at the cited path. The sealed Gemini documents therefore cannot yet be treated as the final, Chairman-ready record.

No candidate code, feed, matrix, Vercel deployment, or production alias change is authorized by this correction.
