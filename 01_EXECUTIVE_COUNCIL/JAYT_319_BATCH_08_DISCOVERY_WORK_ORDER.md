# JAYT-319 — Batch 08 Discovery-only Work Order (N+1)

Data & Trust and QA shall prepare a discovery-only shortlist of official Đà Nẵng public-service leaf pages dated 2025–2026. Each proposal must provide an official URL, a direct article-body text span, a visible source date, a non-commercial utility classification, and a duplication check against the approved registry.

Excluded: homepages, portals without dated leaf content, archived or expired notices, prices, payments, vouchers, affiliate/tracking flows, booking/intake flows, unsupported health or emergency guidance, and any source that would require factual inference beyond its direct body text.

This order authorizes no capture, no raw-file creation, no registry change, no render, and no Production mutation. Each later candidate requires its own CEO scope and one-shot evidence process.
