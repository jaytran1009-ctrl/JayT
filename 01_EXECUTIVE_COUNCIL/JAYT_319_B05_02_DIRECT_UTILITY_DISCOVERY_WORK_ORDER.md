# JAYT-319 — B05_02 Direct Utility Discovery Work Order

Data & Trust may perform documentary discovery only for an official UED leaf page that directly accepts or explains an active student-exchange application: a current eligibility checklist, submission instructions, deadline or application portal is required. The existing MOA signing article remains held and must not be recaptured or rendered.

This work order grants no capture, public approval, rendering, registry mutation, feed mutation or Production action. Any qualifying successor must pass recency and text-span pre-audit before a target-specific CEO scope is considered.
