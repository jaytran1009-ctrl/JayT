# JAYT-319 — Batch 06 Discovery Work Order

Data & Trust and QA shall maintain the N+1 queue by identifying official, dated (2025–2026) leaf pages for non-commercial public utilities in Da Nang across education, civic services, public health and public transport.

Each proposal must provide an official leaf URL, publication/effective-date evidence, one verbatim supporting span, a clear non-commercial card purpose and a duplicate check against the nine-card Staging registry. Exclude homepages, archived events, commercial pricing, appointment/booking flows, and institutional announcements without a direct public utility.

This is discovery only. It authorizes no network capture, raw-file intake, public approval, rendering, registry/feed mutation, or Production action. Every candidate requires a separate pre-audit and target-specific scope.
