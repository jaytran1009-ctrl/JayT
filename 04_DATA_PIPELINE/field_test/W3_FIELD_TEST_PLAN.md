# W3 Field Test Plan — Prepared, Not Yet Recruited

## Objective

Evaluate whether people can complete three client-side tasks without interpreting JayT as an authoritative pricing or affiliate service:

1. Compare a lunch total using values they see in their own delivery app.
2. Run a KTX voucher-stack simulation using self-entered amounts.
3. Find the Spotify Student conditions and DanaBus procedure, including the current-fare limitation.

## Cohort plan

The requested 100-person field cohort is split into 50 students (Bách Khoa and DUE) and 50 office workers near Nguyễn Văn Linh. Recruitment is not started by this plan.

- Conduct 12 consented moderated usability sessions first: 6 students and 6 office workers.
- Run an opt-in, anonymous task-completion survey for the larger cohort only after the moderated session has shown no critical comprehension issue.
- Do not collect names, phone numbers, account data, bank data, location traces, screenshots of personal applications, or purchase history.

## Test tasks and measures

| Task | Success signal | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lunch comparison | Participant enters meal, delivery, and voucher values; identifies the calculated total | No claim that JayT knows live fees, vouchers, or the cheapest provider. |
| KTX simulation | Participant distinguishes a simulation from a redeemable promotion | No live marketplace or affiliate action is tested. |
| Student utilities | Participant identifies Spotify eligibility and the DanaBus fare hold | No current DanaBus fare is inferred or recorded. |
| Zalo Pass | Participant can create a local group-split card | Do not ask participants to share a card or provide group details. |

## Consent and operations

Provide a short purpose statement, voluntary participation notice, and stop/withdraw option before each session. Record only anonymous task outcomes and optional free-text feedback. No outreach, scheduling, or participant contact is authorized by this plan.

## Decision rule

Summarize anonymous outcomes by task and participant segment. Escalate any misleading price interpretation, unclear disclosure, or privacy concern before any future commercial activation.
