    About this template

    Make this your most productive year yet with our Student Starter Pack — our core bundle of templates to stay on top of everything for college and life in one place, made entirely for you to customize.

    What comes in your pack? Your:

    • All-in-one planner: Track to-do’s, assignments, and deadlines.
    • Move-in checklist: Essential items and dorm mood board.
    • Habit tracker: Set and stay on top of routines.
    • Budget tracker: Manage finances and avoid overspending.
    • Resume: Stand out in applications with a personalized website.
    • Club home: Centralize meeting notes, events, and members.
    • Job application tracker: Organize applications and interviews.
    • Dorm life organizer: Manage roommate errands and meal prep.
