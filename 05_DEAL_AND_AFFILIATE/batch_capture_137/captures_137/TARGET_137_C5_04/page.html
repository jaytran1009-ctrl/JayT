Fintech

The Bachelor of Fintech program aims to train and provide high-quality human resources to meet the demands of the labor market and serve the community. In this training program, students will be equipped with political qualities, ethics, professional responsibility, and a sense of social service; possessing broad and deep professional knowledge of finance combined with modern technology. At the same time, the program emphasizes scientific research, knowledge and technology transfer in the field of digital economy and related industries, contributing to promoting sustainable socio-economic development at local, national, and international levels. The program is taught by the Faculty’s lecturers who possess solid expertise and rich teaching experience. In addition, students have opportunities to study with experts from domestic and foreign enterprises, helping them access the latest trends in the Fintech field. Students have many opportunities for internships and participation in international exchange activities at universities collaborating with the University, along with opportunities for articulation studies under 2+2, 1+3, ... programs with international universities.

Students participating in this program study specialized courses in both English and Vietnamese languages. Message of the Rector

VKU with the motto student-centered, pursues the educational philosophy of Humanity - Service – Liberalization to train and foster learners to become comprehensive, honest, and ethical people with a mindset of dynamism, innovation, creativity, and a spirit of being always ready to serve and commit to happiness and the development of the country and humanity based on the core value system: Morality – Mind – Body – Beauty; Prestige – Quality – Professionalism; Inheritance – Innovation – Creation.

Assoc.Prof.Ph.D. Huynh Cong Phap
