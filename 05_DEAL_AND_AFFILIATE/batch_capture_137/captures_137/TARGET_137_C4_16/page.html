Grammarly already helps more than 40 million people write. Now Go can help them learn—and help educators teach. Students can use Go to help them create study guides and research. Educators can use it to help improve campus communications and brainstorm syllabi and lesson plans.  And everyone can use it to reclaim focus and time.

Enterprise security designed for education

Grammarly, now part of Superhuman, provides enterprise-grade security and AI built with education’s specific needs in mind. Customer data and text are never sold or used to train models. Account admins can control whether or not to turn on certain AI features and who within the institution should have access.
