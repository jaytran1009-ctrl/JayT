Frequently Asked Questions

  • Currently, Canva Education is available for K-12 (primary and secondary) students. If you’re a primary or secondary student, your teacher needs to invite you to join Canva Education. They can do this from their Canva Education account – here's how.⁠(opens in a new tab or window)

  • Eligible K-12 teachers and students can access premium features for free, unless disabled by school administrators. Canva Education also includes dedicated features for educators, such as LMS integrations, the ability to share work and assignments with students, thousands of high quality educational templates, and more.


    School districts and institutions can also enjoy enterprise-level security features, deployment via SSO, onboarding support, and professional development training – all for free.

  • Yes – it’s 100% free, with no paywalls or nasty surprises. It’s our commitment that Canva Education will remain 100% free for K-12 (primary and secondary) educators, their students, as well as qualified school districts and institutions.

  • Canva Education is available to students of K–12 (primary or secondary) age. Eligible students can only get access if their teacher invites them. If you’re a teacher, you can do this in your Settings. Learn more about inviting students and teachers⁠(opens in a new tab or window) to your class.

  • Sign up with your school address to check if it matches a school district. If it does, log in to get instant access to Canva Education. If your email doesn't match any school, enter your teacher's email address to let them know your class can access Canva Education for free.

  • Student access to Canva is managed by your school or university. If they offer Canva for Campus, you may be able to access Pro and premium features for free. If not, you can still access Canva Free or Pro at log-in.

  • You could try signing into Canva using your university or college email address. Otherwise, contact your university student services or IT administrators to check if they offer Canva for Campus.

  • Canva Education is available for free for K-12 (primary and secondary) educators, their students, and qualified school districts and institutions. Check out our full eligibility guidelines⁠(opens in a new tab or window). Canva for Campus is our paid offering designed for full campus-wide rollouts at higher education institutions.

  • If you’re graduating this year, you may lose access to your school email as well as access to your free Canva Education or Canva for Campus account. Take your designs with you on this next chapter by copying your designs to your personal account. Here’s how⁠(opens in a new tab or window).
