AI safeguards that schools need, ready from day one

Bring AI to the classroom with total peace of mind. Canva Education gives administrators granular permission controls, with generative AI features disabled by default for students and can only be unlocked by admins.




Certified by iKeepSafe as compliant with FERPA and COPPA, Canva Education helps schools adopt AI safely while keeping teachers and administrators in control. Canva and our third-party providers never use Canva Education content to train AI models.
