Best-in-class creative tools for your Chromebooks

Figma and Google are teaming up to help students transform ideas into reality by bringing Figma’s collaborative design tools directly to Chromebooks—for free.

Get started today
Create together in Figma

Figma is the first design tool built for the web that combines powerful features with multiplayer functionality. Figma makes it faster, easier, and more fun for students to design together—all in one place.

Free and easy

Figma is and always will be free for education. All US K-12 students and educators using Chromebooks receive our Enterprise plan at no cost.

Create from anywhere

Figma is web-based, which means you can natively use Figma with Chromebooks anytime, anywhere—install it with just a few clicks in the App Licensing system.

Work better, together

Interact, add feedback, and update design work in real-time with the whole class—all in one centralized place.

Gain real-world experience

Use industry-leading design tools in the classroom to help students thrive in life after school. Figma is used by some of the world’s most innovative companies—including Google, Spotify, and AirBnB.
