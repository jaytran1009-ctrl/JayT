LOYALTY POINT

We offer 5 membership levels which are: U22 | General | Elite | CGV VIP | CGV VVIP, each offering unique benefits and reward rates.

PointsU22 | General | EliteVIPVVIP
Box Office 5% 7% 10%
E.g: 100.000 VND 5 Points 7 Points 10 Points
Concession 3% 4% 5%
E.g: 100.000 VND 3 Points 4 Points 5 Points

1 point = 1,000 VND, equivalent to cash, which can be redeemed for Drinks, Combos and Movie Tickets at CGV. For example, with a ticket purchase of 100,000 VND, you can:

- Pay 80,000 VND + 20 Points

- Pay 10,000 VND + 90 Points

Rounding Rules:

- From 0.1 to 0.4: round down (For example: 3.2 points are equal to 3 points). Note: transactions having accumulated points from 0.1 to 0.4 will not be accumulated due to rounding down to 0, which means spending will not be accumulated also.

- From 0.5 to 0.9: round up (For example: 3.6 points are equal to 4 points)

Important Notice

1. The expiry of your accumulated points is 01 year. If you get the points at 08:00 on January 10, 2026, your points will expire at 07:59:59 on January 10, 2027.

2. Loyalty points are valid for use at all CGV cinemas nationwide.

3. After the service is completed by CGV Vietnam, loyalty points will be recorded to your account on the next day at 8:00 AM. 

For example: If your showing time takes place on January 10, 2026, your points will be recorded to your account at 8:00 AM January 11, 2026.

4. Spending and loyalty points WILL NOT be accumulated for transactions where discounts or promotions from CGV and its partners have been applied. This includes: CGVian/CJ Cards, Movie Pass, Upgrade Tickets (for VIP/VVIP members), Birthday coupons, and discount codes from CGV or partners (ZaloPay, VNPay, MoMo, Banks, etc.).

5. Spending and loyalty points will NOT be accumulated for the following transactions:

- Purchase of vouchers.

- Purchase of Gift Cards and eGifts.

- Purchase of tickets or F&B using CGVian, CJ membership, or Premium cards.

- Transactions paid 100% with reward->loyalty points.

- Ticket purchases via third-party channels (E-wallets, banks, etc.) that are not made directly through CGV’s website, app, or at the cinema.

For transactions using ONLY Vouchers 

**Excluding other CGV/partner promotions:

- Spending and loyalty points will not be accumulated for tickets where a voucher is applied.

- Spending and loyalty points will only be accumulated for tickets/F&B paid at full price within the same transaction.

**For transactions using both Vouchers and CGV/partner promotions (excluding standard pricing policies for U22, children, seniors, etc.): Spending and loyalty points will NOT be accumulated for the entire transaction.

6. Customers will not be eligible for spending or loyalty point accumulation on transactions that have been refunded or canceled.

7. Members can accumulate points/spending for up to 10 transactions/day.

8. You can easily check your loyalty points and transaction history -> spending accumulation on CGV Website or CGV Mobile App.

9. You must use 10 points at minimum for any transaction.

10. Members when paying online on CGV Website/App, in 1 transaction, points can only be used to pay up to 90% of the transaction value.

11. When making transactions using loyalty points/free tickets directly at CGV cinemas, customers must present their membership account (Physical card/online account on CGV App), or other personal documents including date of birth (original or photo) in order for staff to authenticate the member account owner. CGV staff have the right to refuse the request to redeem the birthday gift if the customer cannot provide the membership account and/or documents regulated herein or the customer's information. Transactions using loyalty points/free tickets on CGV's website/CGV mobile app are assumed to be used by the member account owner. Customers are obliged to keep their Member accounts confidential. CGV Vietnam will not resolve any complaints related to loyalty points if the transactions using those points are performed online.

CGV Vietnam will not refund/resolve used points if the customer cannot prove that he/she is not the person using the points and CGV Vietnam's decision is final.

12. When paying online tickets by points, coupon, voucher, Premium card, CJ member, gift card, the system will ask you to enter a payment code (including 06 digits). For detailed information, please visit HERE.

BIRTHDAY GIFT

Birthday Gift Policy for each CGV Membership Grade:

- General & ELITE & U22: 01 CGV Birthday Combo (01 Popcorn & 02 Drinks).

*Especially, on the 23rd birthday, CGV U22 members will receive 01 free 2D/3D ticket.

- CGV VIP: 01 CGV Birthday Combo + 01 free 2D/3D ticket.

- CGV VVIP: 01 CGV Birthday Combo + 02 free 2D/3D tickets.

(*) CGV Birthday Combo = 01 Popcorn size 44oz + 02 Drinks size 22oz

- To receive your birthday gift, please show us your membership account (Physical membership card or member account on CGV App) and personal documents (ID card). Date of Birth on your ID card needs to be matched with the Date of Birth that you use to register your CGV membership account. If not, our staffs have the right to not give out the CGV birthday gift.

Conditions for receiving birthday gifts:

CGV Birthday Combo will be displayed in the member account who must have a birthday in the month. However, to redeem birthday gift, member must satisfy 01 of the following cases:

- Case 1: Members have made transactions to buy tickets/concessions/Gift card/eGift at the cinema or on the CGV Website/app within the nearest 24 months (excluded the birth month of 02 years ago compared to the current birth month) and customers can go to the cinema to redeem the gift. Example: If your birthday is in January 2026, you must have at least one transaction for tickets, concessions (F&B), or gift cards/ egifts—either at the cinema or via the CGV Website/App—between February 1, 2024, and your current birthday month to be eligible for gift redemption at the cinema.

- Case 2: Member has not made transactions to buy tickets/concessions/Giftcard/eGift at the cinema or on the CGV Website/application within the nearest 24 months, then please make a transaction to buy tickets/concessions/Giftcard/eGift at the cinema or on the CGV Website/application in the birthday month to be eligible to redeem the CGV Birthday Combo IMMEDIATELY.

- Case 3: New members who register and make a transaction to buy tickets/concessions/Giftcard/eGift at the cinema or on the CGV Website/application in the birthday month will be eligible to redeem CGV Birthday Combo IMMEDIATELY.

How to redeem your birthday gift: 

- Customers redeem the birthday gift at the counter at all operating CGV cinemas. 

- In addition, the main account owner please present: (1) Identity Card/VNeID/personal documents showing date of birth and (2) Physical member card/CGV App logged into the account for verification. CGV staff have the right to refuse the request to redeem the birthday gift if the customer cannot provide the membership account and/or documents regulated herein or the customer's information.

Terms details:

1. Expiry using date of birthday gift : Birthday gift coupon is only valid for using during the birthday month (After the birthday month, the coupon will automatically disappear). If your birthday coupon is a movie ticket, you can use it to book showings taking place in the following month, provided that at the time of booking the ticket is still within your birthday month.

2. To receive your birthday gift, please show us: (1) ID Card/VNeID/original personal documents showing the date of birth and (2) Your CGV card/CGV app which is already logged in for verification. Date of Birth (DOB) on your ID card needs to be matched with the DOB that you use to register your CGV membership account. If not, our staffs have the right to not give out the CGV birthday gift.

3. To change the Email Address or Date of Birth information of the membership account, customers must visit the nearest CGV cinema to receive the CUSTOMER INFORMATION CHANGE REQUEST FORM, then fill it out and submit it to the staff at the counter. When visiting, please remember to bring: (1) Identity Card/VNeID/documents showing the date of birth (Original) and (2) Physical member card/CGV App logged into the account needing changes for verification and information storage.

4. Birthday gifts are not redeemable for cash or refundable.

5. After redeeming the birthday gift at the counter, customers cannot request to refund/cancel/receive the birthday gift coupon.

6. Free CGV Combo includes 01 Popcorn size 44oz & 02 Drinks size 22oz, only applied at CGV’s counter. If you want to change the flavor, please pay for the surcharge.

7. For VVIP members who receive 02 free 2D/3D tickets, please note that these tickets can only be redeemed in one transaction at the same time, not applicable for 2 separate transactions.

8. Every free 2D/3D ticket is applied for a general seat or VIP seat in the general auditorium. DO NOT apply for special cinemas, such as 4DX, ULTRA 4DX, IMAX, GOLD GLASS, SWEETBOX, STARIUM, L’AMOUR, PREMIUM, SCREENX, CINE & FORÊT, CINE & LIVING ROOM.

9. Free tickets will be applied for all shows from Monday to Sunday (including holidays) and Early Release but not applied for Sneak Show.

10. The daily birthday gift redemption process at the cinema may be temporarily suspended earlier than scheduled without prior notice, depending on the actual gift inventory remaining for the day at each cinema.

How to view Birthday Coupon information on CGV Web/App:

Website: Log in to cgv.vn Website → Go to “CGV Account” (customer name in the upper right corner of the screen) → Select “Coupon”.

App: Open CGV app → Select “CGV Member” (customer avatar in the upper left corner of the screen) → Select “Gift Card | Voucher | Coupon” → Select “Coupon

11. The "CGV Free Birthday Gift" giveaway applies to all CGV members for all months of the year. Birthday gifts will be notified by CGV to members with birthdays in each respective month.

12. In the case of using the ticket refund feature for transactions that involve a birthday ticket coupon, the coupon will not be reusable.

In the case where one member holds multiple different accounts with the same ID card number, each ID number belonging to the same member may only redeem a maximum of 03 CGV BIRTHDAY COMBOs, 03 Birthday Tickets (VIP, VVIP), and 03 23rd Birthday Tickets within the same day at each cinema.
