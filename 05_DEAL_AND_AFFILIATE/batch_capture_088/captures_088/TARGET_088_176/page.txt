LOYALTY POINT

We offer 5 membership levels which are: U22 | General | Elite | CGV VIP | CGV VVIP, each offering unique benefits and reward rates.

Points	U22 | General | Elite	VIP	VVIP
Box Office	5%	7%	10%
E.g: 100.000 VND	5 Points	7 Points	10 Points
Concession	3%	4%	5%
E.g: 100.000 VND	3 Points	4 Points	5 Points

1 point = 1,000 VND
, equivalent to cash, which can be redeemed for Drinks, Combos and Movie Tickets at CGV. For example, with a ticket purchase of 100,000 VND, you can:

- Pay 80,000 VND + 20 Points

- Pay 10,000 VND + 90 Points

Rounding Rules:

- From 0.1 to 0.4: round down (For example: 3.2 points are equal to 3 points). Note: transactions having accumulated points from 0.1 to 0.4 will not
be
accumulated due to rounding down to 0, which means spending will not be accumulated also.

- From 0.5 to 0.9: round up (For example: 3.6 points are equal to 4 points)

Important Notice

1. The expiry of your accumulated points is 01 year. If you get the points at 08:00 on January 10, 2026, your points will expire at 07:59:59 on January 10, 2027.

2. Loyalty points are valid for use at all CGV cinemas nationwide.

3. After the service is completed by CGV Vietnam, loyalty points will be recorded to your account on the next day at
8:00
AM. 

For example: If your showing time takes place on January 10, 2026, your points will be recorded to your account at 8:00 AM January 11, 2026.

4. Spending and loyalty points WILL NOT be accumulated for transactions where discounts or promotions from CGV and its partners have been applied. This includes: CGVian/CJ Cards, Movie Pass, Upgrade Tickets (for VIP/VVIP members), Birthday coupons, and discount codes from CGV or partners (ZaloPay, VNPay, MoMo, Banks, etc.).

5. Spending and loyalty points will NOT be accumulated for the following transactions:

- Purchase of vouchers.

- Purchase of Gift Cards and eGifts.

- Purchase of tickets or F&B using CGVian, CJ membership, or Premium cards.

- Transactions paid 100% with
reward->loyalty
points.

- Ticket purchases via third-party channels (E-wallets, banks, etc.) that are not made directly through CGV’s website, app, or at the cinema.

For transactions using ONLY Vouchers 

**Excluding other CGV/partner promotions:

- Spending and loyalty points will not be accumulated for tickets where a voucher is applied.

- Spending and loyalty points will only be accumulated for tickets/F&B paid at full price within the same transaction.

**For transactions using both Vouchers and CGV/partner promotions (excluding standard pricing policies for U22, children, seniors, etc.): Spending and loyalty points will NOT be accumulated for the entire transaction.

6. Customers will not be eligible for spending or
loyalty
point accumulation on transactions that have been refunded or canceled.

7. Members can accumulate points/spending for up to 10 transactions/day.

8. You can easily check your loyalty points and
transaction history -> spending accumulation
on CGV Website or CGV
Mobile
App.

9. You must use 10 points at minimum for any transaction.

10. Members when paying online on CGV Website/App, in 1 transaction, points can only be used to pay up to 90% of the transaction value.

11. When making transactions using loyalty points/free tickets directly at CGV cinemas, customers must present their
membership account (Physical card/online account on CGV App)
, or
other personal documents including
date of birth (original or photo) in order for staff to authenticate the member account owner.
CGV staff have the right to refuse the request to redeem the birthday gift if the customer cannot provide the membership account and/or documents regulated herein or the customer's information.
Transactions using loyalty points/free tickets on CGV's website/CGV
mobile
app
are assumed
to be used by the member account owner. Customers are obliged to keep their Member accounts confidential. CGV Vietnam will not resolve any complaints related to loyalty points if the transactions using those points are performed online.

CGV Vietnam will not refund/resolve used points if the customer cannot prove that he/she is not the person using the points and CGV Vietnam's decision is final.

coupon, voucher, Premium card, CJ member, gift card,
