Notion for Education

Students and educators can get Notion Plus plan features for free.

Standard Plus plan pricing: $10/month per user. Student pricing: 100% Free ($0).

Features:

  • Unlimited file uploads (standard free tier has 5MB limit)
  • Unlimited pages and blocks
  • 30-day page history
  • Collaborate with up to 100 guests

Eligibility:

Sign up with or update account to an accredited school/university email domain. Eligible domains automatically activate the free Plus plan in Workspace Settings -> Upgrade -> Get free education plan.
